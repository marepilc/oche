# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — unreleased

First public version.

- X01, Cricket (standard and cut-throat) and Around the Clock for any number of players.
- Training: X01 solo, Checkout, Scoring and Bob's 27.
- Keyboard grammar for entering darts, multi-level undo, checkout hints.
- Game history and statistics: averages over time, first 9, checkout %, heat map, Cricket MPR,
  training results, player comparison, date ranges.
- Local SQLite storage; optional sync to PostgreSQL or MySQL/MariaDB, with import on another computer.
- Colours and font follow the active Omarchy theme.
- English and Polish.
